RagaM-Roll — draw gamakas. Your current composition as a pitch roll — time flows down, pitch across. Tap a note to zoom in, then drag to draw its pitch curve (you hear the pitch as you draw); drag the handles to tweak, snap locks to the 22 shrutis. Right-click a note to copy/paste its gamaka. Play rolls it under a playhead; Share / Load pack the melody + curves into a link.
